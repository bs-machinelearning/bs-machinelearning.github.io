We had the privilege to visit the Microsoft offices in Italy for a full day focused on artificial intelligence, technology, and career development.

The agenda offered a broad range of perspectives, including an overview of Microsoft’s advancements in AI, practical use cases, insights into building a strong tech culture, and an open round table discussion with Microsoft professionals.

We extend our sincere thanks to [Guenda Sciancalepore](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guenda-sciancalepore), [Roberta Bruno](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roberta-bruno-a64476151), and Microsoft Italy for making this visit possible. We are grateful to the entire Microsoft team for their openness and willingness to share valuable insights with us.

We look forward to strengthening the connection between students and the companies shaping the future of technology.
